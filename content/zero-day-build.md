Based on the [Hierarchy of Assets](/hierarchy-of-assets/) and the [Optimal Strategy](/optimal-strategy/), we can derive the two polar extremes of character creation. One build maximizes survival and information dominance; the other maximizes exposure and inevitable destruction.

These are not "roleplaying suggestions." They are the mathematical results of V20's game-theoretic structure.

---

## I. The #1 "Perfect" Build: The Sovereign Ghost
**Archetype:** The Deep-Cover Intelligence Broker
**Clan:** Nosferatu (Structural Invisibility + Information Monopoly)

The goal of this build is **asymmetric agency**. You see everything, you are seen by nothing, and you possess the liquid capital to solve problems before they reach the supernatural layer.

### 1. Attributes (Mental 7 / Social 5 / Physical 3)
- **Mental (Primary):** **Perception 4**, **Intelligence 3**, **Wits 3**. You are a high-resolution sensor.
- **Social (Secondary):** **Manipulation 3**, **Charisma 3**, **Appearance 0**. You bypass the "first impression" trap. Manipulation is your scalpel.
- **Physical (Tertiary):** **Dexterity 2**, **Stamina 2**, **Strength 2**. Baseline survivability.

### 2. Abilities (Talents 13 / Skills 9 / Knowledges 5)
- **Talents (Primary):** **Subterfuge 3**, **Empathy 3**, **Alertness 3**, **Streetwise 3**, **Awareness 1**. You read motives and lies at a master level.
- **Skills (Secondary):** **Stealth 3**, **Etiquette 3**, **Larceny 3**. You move through any environment, social or physical, without friction.
- **Knowledges (Tertiary):** **Investigation 3**, **Finance 2**. You find the data and you own the money.

### 3. Backgrounds (5 Dots)
- **Resources 3**: Total financial sovereignty.
- **Generation 2**: 11th Gen. Higher blood pool and better expenditure ceiling than neonates.

### 4. Disciplines (3 Dots)
- **Obfuscate 2**: The core of the "Be Boring" strategy. If you aren't there, you can't be destroyed.
- **Animalism 1**: Your first-tier intelligence network.

### 5. Merits & Flaws (The Strategic Optimization)
- **Unbondable (5pt Merit)**: Mandatory. You are the only person who controls your utility function.
- **Iron Will (3pt Merit)**: Mandatory. You cannot be Dominanted or Dementated into defecting against your own interests.
- **Sanctity (2pt Merit)**: You appear innocent. This provides social cover for your S-Tier Subterfuge.
- **Deep Sleeper (1pt Flaw)**: A manageable risk for a vampire who is always hidden.
- **Prey Exclusion (1pt Flaw)**: A minor behavioral constraint.

**Freebie Allocation (15 + 2 = 17):**
- **Auspex 1 (7pt)**: Completes the Tier-S information suite.
- **Willpower 8 (2pt)**: High baseline for resistance.
- **Manipulation 4 (4pt)**: Reaches master-level social engineering.
- **Perception 5 (4pt)**: Maximum sensory resolution.

## II. The Absolute Worst Build: The Burning Martyr
**Archetype:** The Glass Cannon Celebrity
**Clan:** Brujah (High Frenzy + Pure Physical Trap)

This build is designed to fail. It maximizes exposure, guarantees Masquerade breaches, and possesses zero defensive or informational capabilities. It is a suicide note in character-sheet form.

### 1. Attributes (Physical 7 / Social 5 / Mental 3)
- **Physical (Primary):** **Strength 4**, **Stamina 3**, **Dexterity 3**. You are a heavy hammer in a world of scalpels.
- **Social (Secondary):** **Appearance 5**, **Charisma 1**, **Manipulation 1**. You are incredibly memorable and famous, but you have no ability to persuade or manipulate.
- **Mental (Tertiary):** **Perception 1**, **Intelligence 1**, **Wits 1**. You are a low-resolution sensor with no ability to process data or react.

### 2. Abilities (Talents 13 / Skills 9 / Knowledges 5)
- **Talents (Primary):** **Brawl 5**, **Intimidation 5**, **Expression 3**. You are violent and loud.
- **Skills (Secondary):** **Athletics 3**, **Drive 3**, **Firearms 3**. High-visibility physical actions.
- **Knowledges (Tertiary):** **Academics 3**, **Occult 2**. Theoretical knowledge with no practical application.

### 3. Backgrounds (5 Dots)
- **Fame 5**: You are a household name. Every hunter in the world has your photo. You cannot hide.
- **Herd 0**: No reliable feeding. You must hunt manually in a city where everyone knows your face.

### 4. Disciplines (3 Dots)
- **Potence 2**, **Celerity 1**. Pure combat power. No information, no concealment, no control.

### 5. Merits & Flaws (The Final Nails)
- **Blood Hunted (6pt Flaw)**: You start the game as a target for every vampire in the sect.
- **Addiction (3pt Flaw)**: You are physically dependent on a substance, creating a leash for any elder to pull.
- **Monstrous (3pt Flaw)**: You have Appearance 0 (despite the Attribute points), ensuring zero social flexibility.
- **Short Fuse (2pt Flaw)**: You frenzy at the slightest provocation, maximizing Masquerade breaches.

**Freebie Allocation (15 + 14 = 29):**
- **Brawl 5 / Intimidation 5**: Sinking points into the physical failure state.
- **Strength 5**: Maximizing the hammer.
- **Humanity 1**: You are a hair's breadth from becoming a mindless beast.

## The Comparison

The **Sovereign Ghost** enters the city, gathers every secret, owns the mayor's debt, and is never officially registered as existing. They survive centuries.

The **Burning Martyr** walks into a bar, frenzies because someone looked at their famous face, kills three mortals in front of cameras, and is executed by the Sheriff before the first sunrise.

**Choose carefully.**
