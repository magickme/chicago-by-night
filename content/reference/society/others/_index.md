Factions that operate outside or alongside the main Sect framework — either as independent vampiric political bodies with their own traditions, or as non-Cainite supernaturals whose existence intersects with Kindred affairs.

| Page | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Ashirra](/reference/society/others/ashirra/) | Dominant vampiric body of the Islamic world — North Africa, Middle East, Central Asia |
| [Tal'Mahe'Ra](/reference/society/others/tal-mahera/) | The True Black Hand — covert meta-Sect with agents embedded inside all factions |
| [Inconnu](/reference/society/others/inconnu/) | Ancient Kindred withdrawn from the Jyhad; Monitors; Golconda connection |
| [Laibon](/reference/society/others/laibon/) | Vampiric peoples of sub-Saharan Africa; ten Legacies; independent of Western politics |
| [External Factions](/reference/society/others/external-factions/) | Non-Cainite supernaturals: Kuei-jin, Lupines, Fae, Mages |
