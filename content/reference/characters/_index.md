Chicago Chronicles follows two player characters operating in tandem across the southern Lake Michigan corridor.

**Darius Cole** — Ventrue 10th generation (cover 12th), Gary Indiana, 1989. Power and paranoia in equal measure. A debt-collector of blood and favors.

**Sable Price** — Toreador 9th generation, Chicago Act II onward. Art, survival, and the long game. A predator who moves like a socialite.

Both share a haven. Neither checks in by payphone.
