## Feeding

No feeding restriction. Sable can feed from any mortal. Primary methods: seduction (pool 8), social engineering (pool 7).

**Caution:** Appearance 5 means vessels remember the encounter vividly. Repeated feeding in the same area builds a recognition risk.

## Key Capabilities

- **Appearance 5** — The highest attribute rating. Creates immediate social attention and memorable impressions. Cuts both ways.
- **Presence 3 (Entrancement)** — Scene-ending social control at pool 8. Combined with Aura Perception (when active), makes her the primary social-threat-assessment tool.
- **Auspex 3 (Spirit's Touch)** — Recently acquired. Investigation and forensic capability that Darius lacks entirely.
- **Stealth 5** — The most mobile PC in dangerous situations.
- **Low Humanity (5)** — Beast closer to surface. Conscience 2 means degeneration checks are difficult. Higher courage provides some compensation.

## Notes

- **Clan weakness:** Susceptible to Entrancement. Any Presence-user with Entrancement can potentially affect Sable unless she makes SC rolls.
- **Aura Perception burned** until Dec 10, 1990. Van man's aura: permanently blocked.
- Spirit's Touch is new — still developing intuition for what it returns and when.
