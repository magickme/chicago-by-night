## Feeding Restriction

Darius feeds only from **mortals who owe debts they cannot pay**. All other blood provides zero nourishment — vomited immediately. The target's qualifying status must be confirmed before approaching.

Operationally: Darius maintains a network of debtors across Gary. New cities require building equivalent leverage or finding proxies who can identify qualifying vessels.

## Key Capabilities

- **Dominate 4** — Full conditioning capability. The most powerful social toolset available without Presence at high dots.
- **Social pools 5–6** — Manipulation-heavy. Deception and indirect pressure preferred over direct confrontation.
- **Streetwise 3** — Navigates Gary territory and underworld without friction. Weaker in new cities until contacts are established.
- **Firearms 1** — .357 snub-nose. Combat-capable but not optimized for it.
- **SC capped by BP** — When blood-thin, emotional regulation collapses first.
