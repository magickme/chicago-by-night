## Initiative

Roll **1d10 + Dex + Wits**. Highest total acts first. Ties broken by Wits; further ties simultaneous.

In solo play: one roll per side for speed. NPCs act on the GM-rolled initiative.

## Attack

**Roll: Dex + Brawl / Melee / Firearms, diff 6.**

Each net success = the attack lands. Extra successes beyond 1 add +1 damage die each.

**Targeting:** Attacking a specific location is +1 diff. Called shots to the heart (for staking) are diff 9.

## Damage

**Roll damage dice (equal to weapon rating + any bonus dice from successes).**

Each success on the damage roll = 1 health level. Damage type determines what soaks it.

| Attack type | Damage die base | Type |
|-------------|-----------------|------|
| Unarmed (punch) | Str | Bashing |
| Kick | Str + 1 | Bashing |
| Knife | Str + 1 | Lethal |
| Stake | Str + 1 | Lethal |
| .357 revolver | 4 | Lethal |
| Bite (grappled) | Str + 1 | Aggravated |
| Fire | varies | Aggravated |
| Sunlight | varies | Aggravated |

## Soaking

**Roll Stamina (+ Fortitude if possessed), diff 6.** Each success absorbs 1 health level.

| Damage type | Can soak with |
|-------------|---------------|
| Bashing | Stamina (halved vs vampires anyway) |
| Lethal | Stamina |
| Aggravated | Fortitude only |

Vampires halve Bashing damage before soaking (round down). Mortals cannot soak Lethal or Aggravated.

## Bite

Requires a successful **Grapple** first (see below). Once grappled: Str + 1 Aggravated damage, automatic hit, no separate attack roll needed. Victim experiences The Kiss unless resisting with WP.

## Staking

Target: **Dex + Melee, diff 9** (heart). On a hit: 3 or more damage past soak = paralysis. Not Final Death — paralysis only, until the stake is removed. Incapacitates a vampire until staked out in sunlight or beheaded.

## Grapple

**Initiate:** Str + Brawl, diff 6. Opposed — defender rolls same. Net successes determine control.

Once grappled, each subsequent turn: winner re-rolls to maintain or increase control. Grappled characters cannot take other actions without first breaking free (spending a turn on a contested Str + Brawl roll).

## Multiple Opponents

Each additional opponent beyond the first adds **+1 difficulty** to all rolls (maximum +4).

## Wound Penalties

All dice pools suffer penalties equal to health level severity (see [Core Dice → Health Levels](../core-dice/)). Spend 1 WP to ignore penalties for 1 turn.
