All seven founding clans belong to the Camarilla (1990 — Brujah and Gangrel have not yet departed). Sabbat clans operate outside this framework.

## Camarilla Clans

| Clan | Nickname | In-Clan Disciplines | Weakness |
|------|----------|---------------------|----------|
| **Ventrue** | Blue Bloods | Dominate, Fortitude, Presence | Feed only on one specific mortal type chosen at Embrace (permanent). All other mortal or animal blood provides zero nourishment and is immediately vomited. Vampiric blood is exempt. |
| **Toreador** | Degenerates | Auspex, Celerity, Presence | When encountering something genuinely beautiful — art, person, scene — roll Self-Control diff 6 or become entranced for the remainder of the scene. Cannot act; only freed by physical intervention, WP spend, or the beauty being removed. |
| **Brujah** | Rabble | Celerity, Potence, Presence | Frenzy difficulty +2 always. Cannot spend WP to avoid Frenzy (may spend 1 WP to end one already in progress). |
| **Gangrel** | Outlanders | Animalism, Fortitude, Protean | Gain one animal trait per Frenzy — eyes, ears, fur patch, claws, etc. After 5+ traits, Social rolls with non-Gangrel at +1 diff per trait over 4. Traits can be removed via XP. |
| **Nosferatu** | Sewer Rats | Animalism, Obfuscate, Potence | Appearance 0, permanent. Cannot raise Appearance. All Social rolls relying on first impressions fail automatically unless Obfuscate is active. |
| **Malkavian** | Lunatics | Auspex, Dementation, Obfuscate | Permanent, incurable derangement chosen at Embrace. Cannot be cured by any means — not WP, not therapy, not Thaumaturgy. The derangement shapes all behavior. |
| **Tremere** | Warlocks | Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy | A Tremere drinking another vampire's vitae reaches blood bond stage faster: the first drink counts as two stages. After only **two drinks**, the Tremere is fully bonded. This vulnerability to bonding makes them susceptible to elder control. |

## Independent Clans

| Clan | Nickname | In-Clan Disciplines | Weakness / Notes |
|------|----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Followers of Set** | Setites | Obfuscate, Presence, Serpentis | −1 die on all rolls in direct sunlight or bright artificial light. Worship Set; treat all other religions as corruption sources. |
| **Giovanni** | — | Dominate, Necromancy, Potence | The Embrace causes twice the normal damage as a painful blood drain (no other effect). Politically neutral; avoid Camarilla and Sabbat entanglements. |
| **Ravnos** | Deceivers | Animalism, Chimerstry, Fortitude | Must roll Self-Control (diff 6) to resist acting on a specific vice tied to clan identity. Each night without indulging increases the difficulty by 1. |
| **Assamite** | Assassins | Celerity, Obfuscate, Quietus | Bound by the Tremere Curse (1999+, but 1990 version still applies): cannot diablerize other Kindred; doing so causes the consumed blood to turn to ash. |
| **Lasombra** | — | Dominate, Obtenebration, Potence | No reflection in mirrors; Auspex can detect their presence. Primarily Sabbat. |
| **Tzimisce** | Fiends | Animalism, Auspex, Vicissitude | Must sleep in at least two handfuls of soil from their homeland or a place of significance. Failure = lose 1 die from all pools until the next sleep in proper soil. Primarily Sabbat. |

## Caitiff

**Clanless** — no clan, no sire support, no in-clan Discipline prices. XP cost for all Disciplines is out-of-clan rate. Frequently hunted or marginalized; no Primogen to speak for them. Treated as suspect by all factions.

**Generation and clan disciplines interact:** a 10th-gen Ventrue can only spend 1 BP/turn, limiting Discipline use to 1 activation per action. A 5th-gen elder with Celerity can activate multiple times per round, burning blood at a rate lower-generation Kindred cannot match.
