## Dominate (Darius, 4 dots)

Ventrue clan Discipline. Requires eye contact for all powers. Cannot Dominate fellow Ventrue.

### Command (••)

> **Pool:** Manipulation 4 vs target Willpower

Bark a single word or brief command. Target must obey immediately — one physical action. Command must be possible to execute. Target does not remember being Commanded if they succeed a WP roll (diff 6) afterward.

### Mesmerize (•••)

> **Pool:** Manipulation 4 + Intimidation 2 (6 dice) vs target Willpower

Implant a suggestion, instruction, or behavioral compulsion. Eye contact required. Trigger can be delayed ("when you see X, do Y"). Target resists by spending WP (diff 6) — one WP negates the effect for one scene, repeated spending required for permanent resistance. Successes determine depth of the command.

### Forgetful Mind (••••)

> **Pool:** Manipulation 4 + Subterfuge 2 (5 dice) vs target Willpower

Rewrite, alter, or erase memories. Success level determines precision:

| Successes | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Haze — period feels vague, details gone |
| 2 | Scene erased cleanly |
| 3 | Full rewrite possible — new memories implanted |
| 5 | Granite — permanent, resists later Domination attempts to restore |

### Conditioning (•••••)

> **Pool:** Manipulation 4 + Intimidation 2 (6 dice) vs target Willpower — **extended action, 14 total successes required**

Permanent behavioral modification. The target becomes conditioned to obey Darius without WP resistance or memory gaps. Requires multiple sessions of sustained contact. Once achieved: the target treats Darius's instructions as their own desires.

## Presence (Darius 1, Sable 3)

Toreador/Ventrue Discipline. Emotional manipulation at range — no eye contact required unless specified.

### Awe (•) — Both PCs

> **Pool:** Charisma + Performance, diff 7

Everyone in the room becomes inclined favorably toward the user for the scene. This is not control — just positive disposition. Targets are more receptive, less aggressive, more willing to listen.

### Dread Gaze (••) — Sable

> **Pool:** Charisma 4 + Intimidation 1 (5 dice) vs target Willpower

Overwhelming terror. Target flees or cowers for the rest of the scene (or until the source leaves). Mortals who fail badly may suffer lingering trauma. Does not work on those with True Faith 4+.

### Entrancement (•••) — Sable

> **Pool:** Appearance 5 + Empathy 3 (8 dice) vs target Willpower

Total fascination. Target follows, agrees, adores for the scene duration. Broken by a direct physical threat to the target. **Sable's clan weakness:** she herself is susceptible to Entrancement — SC 4 vs diff 6 to resist.

## Auspex (Sable, 3 dots)

Toreador Discipline. Perceptive enhancement and psychic sensing.

### Heightened Senses (•)

> **Pool:** Perception 3 + Alertness 2 (5 dice) — activated, lasts scene

Enhance one sense dramatically. Can hear whispered conversations across rooms, smell blood in veins, see in near-total darkness. Overstimulation risk: if exposed to sudden loud noise, bright light, or overwhelming smell while active, roll Sta diff 6 or be disoriented for one turn.

### Aura Perception (••)

> **Pool:** Perception 3 + Empathy 3 (6 dice), diff 8

Read emotional state, supernatural nature, and deception. Auras are color-coded — each color maps to an emotional state or creature type.

**Current status: BURNED until December 10, 1990.** Sable burned this power reading the van man (D031). Additionally: the van man's aura is **permanently blocked** — Beast flinch response. Attempting to read that specific individual again requires a Courage roll diff 8 first. All other subjects function normally.

### Spirit's Touch (•••)

> **Pool:** Perception 3 + Empathy 3 (6 dice), diff varies — recent/significant = easier

Touch an object to read psychic impressions left by previous holders. Returns emotional impressions, fragmentary images, and sensory memory. Difficulty scales with age of impressions and emotional significance: a knife used in a murder hours ago = diff 5. A coin passed through a thousand hands over decades = diff 9.

**Newly acquired.** First use was in scene D031.

## Discipline Burnout (Campaign Ruling)

Using Auspex on a True Faith bearer and botching = burnout lasting (Faith rating) nights for that specific power. Other Auspex powers function normally during burnout — the burn is channeled-specific, not system-wide.

Generalizes: botching Dominate or Presence against a True Faith 4+ target may similarly burn that power against that specific individual.
