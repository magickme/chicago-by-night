## Humanity

Humanity measures how much of the mortal self remains intact. Range: 0–10.

| Humanity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| 10 | Saintly — near-impossible to maintain |
| 8–9 | Humane — functional moral compass |
| 6–7 | Average — some callousness |
| 4–5 | Callous — difficulty relating to mortals |
| 2–3 | Predatory — near-feral, dehumanizing |
| 1 | Monstrous — barely coherent moral sense |
| 0 | Wight — lost to the Beast, NPC |

### Degeneration Checks

When a vampire commits an act that violates their current Humanity level's Hierarchy of Sins, they must roll:

> **Pool:** Conscience rating, difficulty 8. Failure = lose 1 Humanity permanently.

The Hierarchy of Sins is tiered — acts at or below the current Humanity level require checks. Acts above the current level do not (already too far gone to feel them). See [Paths of Enlightenment](paths/) for the full Hierarchy table and Path variants.

## Frenzy

The Beast emerges when stress exceeds Self-Control. Frenzy = temporary loss of rational control — the vampire acts on pure predatory instinct. See [Frenzy & Rotschreck](frenzy/) for the full trigger table and resistance rules.

### Beast Voice

When BP falls to half or below the pool maximum: consider writing one italicized sentence per scene voicing the Beast's commentary — hunger, rage, predatory assessment. The Beast never offers solutions; it narrows options.

> *The man smelled of blood and fear. The Beast noted: he would be easy.*

---

## Rotschreck

The Frenzy of fear — triggered by fire, sunlight, True Faith. The vampire flees, cannot act rationally until the source is removed.

> **Pool:** Courage, difficulty varies. Fire: diff 3–8 (intensity-dependent). Sunlight: diff 7–10. True Faith: diff equals Faith rating.

---

## The Beast Pool (Narrative Tool)

Beast pressure = **10 − Humanity**. Not a mechanical pool — a narrative measure of how easily the Beast finds purchase. A vampire at Humanity 4 has a Beast pool of 6; the Beast is closer to the surface, sharper, harder to deny.
