## The Dual Axis

VtM play operates on two simultaneous tracks:

- **External:** Power accumulation, domain, status, survival.
- **Internal:** Humanity erosion, Beast encroachment, moral degeneration.

Both are always in play. Scenes that produce external gains often cost internal stability. The best play engages both axes in every session.

---

## Humanity

Humanity measures how much of the mortal self remains intact. Range: 0–10. Starting values vary by PC history.

| Humanity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| 10 | Saintly — near-impossible to maintain |
| 8–9 | Humane — functional moral compass |
| 6–7 | Average — some callousness |
| 4–5 | Callous — difficulty relating to mortals |
| 2–3 | Predatory — near-feral, dehumanizing |
| 1 | Monstrous — barely coherent moral sense |
| 0 | Wight — lost to the Beast |

**Current values:** Darius H7, Sable H5.

### Degeneration Checks

When a vampire commits an act that violates their current Humanity level's hierarchy of sins, they must roll:

> **Pool:** Conscience rating, diff 8. Failure = lose 1 Humanity permanently.

Darius: Conscience 3 (3 dice). Sable: Conscience 2 (2 dice).

The hierarchy of sins is tiered — acts at or below the current Humanity level require checks. Acts above the current level do not (already too far gone to feel them).

## Frenzy

The Beast emerges when stress exceeds Self-Control. Frenzy = temporary loss of rational control — the vampire acts on pure predatory instinct.

### Triggers and Difficulties

| Trigger | Diff |
|---------|------|
| Smell of blood (hungry) | 3+ |
| Sight of blood (hungry) | 4+ |
| Harassment, taunts, or life threat | 4 |
| Physical provocation | 6 |
| Taste of blood (hungry) | 6+ |
| Loved one in danger | 7 |
| Public humiliation | 8 |

**Brujah:** Add +2 to all Frenzy difficulties.

### Frenzy Resistance

> **Pool:** Self-Control, diff as above. 5 successes = overcome Frenzy entirely. Failure = full Frenzy for the scene.

**SC cap:** SC pool is capped by current BP. BP 2 = max 2 SC dice. See [Blood Economy](../blood-economy/).

**Frenzy resolution:** Binary choice, no good option. The Beast doesn't offer solutions — it offers release. The narrative should reflect this: the choice between Frenzy and whatever it takes to stop Frenzy is the scene.

### Beast Voice

When BP ≤ half max rating: write one italicized blockquote per substantial scene response, voicing the Beast's commentary — hunger, rage, predatory assessment. The Beast is never helpful. It observes, pressures, and narrows options.

> *The man smelled of blood and fear. The Beast noted: he would be easy.*

## Rotschreck (Terror Frenzy)

The Frenzy of fear — triggered by fire, sunlight, True Faith. The vampire flees, cannot act rationally until the source is out of range.

> **Pool:** Courage, diff varies. Fire: diff 3–5 (intensity-dependent). Sunlight: diff 8–10. True Faith: diff equals Faith rating.

Darius: Courage 4. Sable: Courage 5.

Unlike Frenzy, Rotschreck *can* be triggered intentionally by opponents with True Faith or fire. Dane's True Faith 5 makes proximity to him a constant Rotschreck risk for both PCs.

## Beast Pool (Frenzy Resolution Narrative)

> Beast pool = 10 − Humanity. Darius: 3. Sable: 5.

This is not a mechanical pool — it's a narrative measure of how easily the Beast finds purchase. Sable's lower Humanity means the Beast is closer to the surface: sharper, more intrusive, harder to deny.
