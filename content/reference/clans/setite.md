The Followers of Set are a clan and a religion simultaneously. They worship Set — the Egyptian god of chaos, storms, and the underworld — and regard their vampiric condition as a gift from their patron. Their mission, as they understand it, is to corrupt: to find what every being values most and introduce the seed of its destruction. They are patient, charming, and deeply invested in the long-term degradation of everything they touch.

**Nickname:** Setites, Serpents

**Sect:** Independent

**In-Clan Disciplines:** Obfuscate, Presence, Serpentis

## Weakness

**Children of Darkness:** Setites take an additional level of aggravated damage from sunlight. Even indirect sunlight in significant quantities (a bright overcast day) is painful. Additionally, bright artificial light causes the same unease that direct sunlight creates for other Kindred — difficulty penalties when in brightly lit environments.

This means Setites are most functional at night and avoid well-lit spaces even indoors. Security lighting, police flashlights, stadium lights, and photography flash all create problems.

## Clan Culture

**The Temples:** Setite operations are centered on Temples — not religious buildings in the conventional sense, but service organizations that provide what people want most and can't get elsewhere. Drugs, sex, forbidden knowledge, weapons, specific services that no one else will perform. The Temple draws the desperate and the corrupt.

**The long game:** Setites do not typically destroy their targets immediately. They build relationships, provide services, create dependency, and then leverage. The goal is corruption — not just what someone will do for the Setite, but what they become in the process.

**Genuine religion:** Many Setites are devout. They believe Set will return from his imprisonment at the end of days and reclaim the world. Their work of corruption is preparation — clearing the ground, weakening opposition, building the infrastructure of Set's eventual dominion. This faith is not pretense; it structures everything they do.

**Preservation of lore:** Paradoxically, the Followers of Set are among the best-preserved sources of ancient esoteric knowledge. Their temples hold texts that exist nowhere else. Scholars who want this information have to deal with Setites — which is, of course, the point.

## Roleplaying Notes

A Setite character who simply runs a drug operation is playing the archetype without its depth. The interesting version is a Setite who genuinely believes in the mission — who views their corruption of others as both service and theology, and who has thought carefully about the line between providing what people want and manufacturing the want itself.

The light sensitivity weakness makes every environment a consideration. A Setite player quickly develops awareness of light sources and contingency plans.
