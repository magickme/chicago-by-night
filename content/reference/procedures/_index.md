Procedures define the operational rhythm of play. They exist to ensure nothing is forgotten between sessions and that every scene starts from an accurate game state.

The core principle: **canonical state lives in files, not in memory**. Session-state.md is authoritative. The boot packet is derived. If they diverge, regenerate the boot packet.
