Creating a vampire character in V20 follows five sequential steps. Dots allocated during creation represent what your character has developed since the Embrace; experience points represent growth during play.

## The Five Steps

### Step 1 — Concept

Choose:
- **Concept:** A one-line encapsulation of who this character is. Not what they do — who they are. ("Broken idealist," "ambitious debt collector," "artist whose hunger ate her compassion.")
- **Clan:** Your vampiric lineage. Determines in-Clan Disciplines, weakness, and political affiliations. See [Clans](clans/).
- **Nature:** Your true, innermost personality archetype. Determines how you regain Willpower. See [Archetypes](archetypes/).
- **Demeanor:** The face you show the world. May differ radically from Nature. Also an archetype.

### Step 2 — Select Attributes

Attributes are divided into three categories: **Physical** (Strength, Dexterity, Stamina), **Social** (Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance), and **Mental** (Perception, Intelligence, Wits).

Prioritize: one category is Primary (7 dots to distribute), one Secondary (5 dots), one Tertiary (3 dots). Every attribute starts with 1 free dot before you spend these.

Maximum at creation: 5. No single attribute may exceed this.

### Step 3 — Select Abilities

Abilities are divided into: **Talents** (intuitive), **Skills** (trained), and **Knowledges** (studied).

Prioritize: Primary (13 dots), Secondary (9 dots), Tertiary (5 dots). No Ability may exceed 3 dots at creation without spending freebie points.

### Step 4 — Select Advantages

- **Disciplines:** 3 dots, distributed among your Clan's in-Clan Disciplines. Caitiff choose any three Disciplines.
- **Backgrounds:** 5 dots among any applicable Backgrounds. See [Backgrounds](backgrounds/).
- **Virtues:** 7 dots distributed among Conscience (or Conviction), Self-Control (or Instinct), and Courage. Each Virtue starts with 1 free dot.

### Step 5 — Finishing Touches

- **Humanity** = Conscience + Self-Control (starting value; lowers during play)
- **Willpower** = Courage rating (permanent Willpower)
- **Blood Pool** = roll 1d10 (starting vitae in your veins)
- **Spend 15 Freebie Points** (see table below)
- **Take Merits and Flaws** (optional; see [Merits & Flaws](merits-flaws/))

## Freebie Point Costs

| Trait | Cost per Dot |
|-------|-------------|
| Attribute | 5 |
| Ability | 2 |
| Discipline (in-Clan) | 7 |
| Background | 1 |
| Virtue | 2 |
| Humanity/Path | 2 |
| Willpower | 1 |

Merits and Flaws exchange freebie points for advantages and disadvantages (max 7 Flaw points taken at creation).

## Experience Point Costs (During Play)

| Purchase | Cost |
|----------|------|
| New Ability (0 → 1) | 3 |
| New in-Clan Discipline (0 → 1) | 10 |
| New out-of-Clan Discipline (0 → 1) | 15 |
| New Path of Sorcery / Necromancy (0 → 1) | 7 |
| Attribute | current × 4 |
| Ability | current × 2 |
| In-Clan Discipline | current × 5 |
| Out-of-Clan Discipline | current × 7 |
| Secondary Path (Thaumaturgy/Necromancy) | current × 4 |
| Virtue (Conscience, Self-Control, Courage) | current × 2 |
| Humanity or Path | current × 2 |
| Willpower | current rating |

## Experience Awards

**Per chapter (3–5 scenes):**

| Award | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| Automatic | 1 XP for completing the chapter |
| Learning Curve | Character learned something significant |
| Roleplaying | Character was played consistently and compellingly |
| Heroism | Exceptional risk taken for a genuine goal |

**Per story (3–5 chapters):**

| Award | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| Success | The primary story goal was achieved |
| Danger | The character survived significant peril |
| Wisdom | The character made a meaningful, costly choice |

**Chicago Chronicles rates:** 3 XP per chapter automatic; bonus for genuine risk. Phantom XP (3 pts toward Humanity only) for genuine in-character moral confession to coterie-mate.
