## 1991 — Act I: Forged in Steel (Gary)

### December 31, 1990 — Campaign Start

NYE going into 1991. First scenes. Both PCs active.

### January

| Week | Darius | Sable |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 (Jan 1-7) | Dock scouting. Ray Pulaski cultivation. First encounters with Gary Kindred. | The Torch. Feeding. First court impressions. |
| 2 (Jan 8-14) | Pipeline deepens. Shipment observation. Dane surveillance at Telton Cemetery. | Haven established. Modius claims Sable publicly at Elysium. |
| 3-4 (Jan 15-31) | Dock intelligence: elder Gangrel in soil, Soviet weapons, Odessa network. Gregory Stephens sighted. | Allicia displacement arc begins. Court routine. |

### February-March

Sandbox period. Threat clocks ticking. First report to Chuc Luc. Dock Pipeline Exposure clock climbing. Dane continues building his case.

*Feb 24 — Gulf War ground offensive begins. National attention diverted from Gary.*

### April-May

Spring thaw. Gary stirs. Blood at Dawn target window opens. Dane escalation: Gideon Ministries ramps up surveillance.

*Late April — Gulf War ends. No economic benefit for Gary.*

### June-August

Summer. Peak activity. Blood at Dawn should resolve by midsummer. Modius demands intensify at court sessions. Allicia subplot develops.

### September-October

Final sandbox escalation. Resolve or advance Act I threads before Baptism by Fire. Annabelle seeds: word reaches Gary about tensions with Chicago.

### November

Pre-holiday tension. Modius begins planning his NYE party. Threat clocks should be near critical.

*Nights: ~15 hours of darkness. Maximum operating time for a vampire.*

### December 31, 1991 — BAPTISM BY FIRE

The act break. Modius's mansion. The party. The letter. The mission to Chicago.
