## The Story

Denver, 1992. You woke in a burning basement. You were mortal yesterday.

A man named Jacob Prestor spent a century trying to reverse vampirism through science. He created you — not through the traditional Embrace but through biochemical injection — and then died before he could explain any of it. Thaddeus, a Nosferatu ancient old enough to remember the fall of Rome, burned the building and departed. The building is on fire. And somewhere in the city is a serum that might be able to undo what was done to you.

This is a standalone chronicle set in Denver, using the *Alien Hunger* (WW2100) jumpstart adventure as source material. It runs parallel to the Chicago Chronicles but shares no characters, setting, or continuity.

---

## The Player Character

**[Theresa "Terri" Harper](/theresa-harper/)** — Gangrel, 13th generation. Chemistry teacher and co-founder of a small tech consulting firm. The serum-creation process transformed her on the night of November 14, 1992. She has Science 4 and can read exactly what was done to her. That's either an advantage or a different kind of horror.
