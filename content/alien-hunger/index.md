## The Story

Denver, February 1991. You woke in a burning basement. You were mortal a day ago.

A bearded man named Jacob Prestor spent a century convinced he could reverse what had been done to him through chemistry. He was wrong, and he didn't know he was wrong. What he actually invented was a way to make new vampires by injection — and then he made you, four other strangers, and three mortals he kept for reasons he never lived to explain.

Thaddeus — a Nosferatu old enough to remember the fall of Rome — burned the building and walked away. The man who would have explained any of this is dead. The building is on fire. And somewhere in the city is a vial Prestor believed could make you human again.

This chronicle runs in parallel to the Chicago Chronicles. Same world. Same nights. Different city, different blood.

## The Player Character

**Arnold "Flash" Simpson** — Brujah, 13th generation, serum-created. Ex-college football star turned sales rep. Coarse, charming, used to getting what he wants by walking through the front door. He woke up on a concrete floor with a hunger he doesn't have words for and four other strangers in the same condition. He's the one who's going to stand up first.
