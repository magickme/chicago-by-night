*Gary has no harpies. Enforcement is collective memory, reputation, and princely intervention. Debts are traded like baseball cards. Updated: **July 19, 1990.***

<div class="boon-ref">
<strong>Boon Levels</strong><br>
1 — <strong>Trivial:</strong> Delivery, unsolicited info, small introduction<br>
2 — <strong>Minor:</strong> Help carry out plan, solicited info, professional service<br>
3 — <strong>Moderate:</strong> Aid in battle, help out of trouble, significant risk<br>
4 — <strong>Major:</strong> Direct intervention in danger, major political cover<br>
5 — <strong>Life:</strong> Saving unlife<br><br>
<em>"Granting favor = strength. Asking = weakness."</em> In Gary, a Minor boon is worth a Moderate anywhere else. Scarcity inflates everything.
</div>

*The boon economy in Gary runs on scarcity and memory. Eight Kindred tracking debts without harpies, without a formal ledger, without the infrastructure that makes prestation work in Chicago. Every favor given is a favor that could have been hoarded. Every debt unpaid is a reputation that erodes. The margins are thinner here. The memory is longer.*
