One of the high priests of an independent Mithraic cell. In 1969 she is active and running cult operations in London. Her faith is real but not uncomplicated -- the questions she asks about Mithras are the kind that get people killed or promoted, depending on who's listening.

In 2012 (Fall of London) she goes missing and may have been placed on the Red List. Her childe is killed by Operation Antigen. None of that has happened yet. In 1969, she is simply a believer doing the work.

**Image:** Welsh. Reserved. The kind of stillness that comes from decades of ritual discipline.

**Apparent Age:** Indeterminate
