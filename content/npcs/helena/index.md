Toreador Methuselah. Childe of Minos. 4th generation. Three thousand two hundred years old. Even just coming out of torpor, one of the most beautiful creatures in existence. She still has dirt in her hair. Goes by the name Portia.

## Origin

Around 1200 B.C., Helena was the most beautiful of Achaean women. Favorite daughter of the king of the coastal city of Argos. Then Minos came to visit — an ancient, horribly ugly Kindred who fell in love with her immediately. When Helena begged her father to drive the creature out, she watched his eyes glaze over as he told her she was going to marry the old man. She fled the palace with a single handmaiden.

On the shore of the Aegean she met Prince Prias — the most beautiful man she had ever seen. He convinced her to flee to his city in Asia Minor. For ten years they lived happily with Prias's noble family. Then Minos tracked them down. He tore through Prias's ancestral home, hurled Prias through a wall, and seized Helena.

Minos chained her to her father's bed and fed from her for months — taking slightly more blood than her body could replace, prolonging the agony. On the last night he drained her completely, replaced her blood with just enough of his own, and left her locked in a room with her aged father. When her first Frenzy subsided, Helena realized she had killed him.

Her spirit broken, she allowed the marriage. She placed the crown on Minos's head herself. But she never stopped plotting. At Delphi, the oracle told her that drinking her sire's blood could destroy him and make her more powerful — but would consume what remained of her soul. For thirteen years she waited. Then Prias returned with soldiers. He drove a wooden spear into the sleeping Minos's breast. As the ancient lay paralyzed, Helena sprang for her sire's throat. She drank deeply. She committed the Amaranth — diablerie of one's own sire.

Prias refused the Embrace but accepted the blood. They remained together for centuries as Kindred and ghoul.

## The War

At Carthage, Helena and Prias were caught up in the Brujah dream. When Rome moved against it, they saw who the winners would be. They fled to Rome and gave the Ventrue the intelligence they needed to destroy the city. In exchange, Helena received the fief of Pompeii.

One 4th-generation Brujah survived the massacre: Menele. He discovered Helena's betrayal and swore vengeance. He tracked her to Pompeii and summoned a spirit of fire to destroy her — losing control, the eruption buried the city. Helena survived through Prias's aid. They fled to Egypt.

For 1,300 years: pursuit, battle, flight. Spain to the New World. Helena manipulated the Conquistadors to destroy Menele's allies — the Aztecs, the Maya, the Inca. She allied with Cortez, then Pizarro, burning civilizations to flush out a single adversary. Menele fled north through the Pueblos.

In 1820, Kansas. In 1832, Fort Dearborn. Helena allied with the United States military. Menele with Chief Black Hawk. Both Methuselahs dealt each other crippling blows. Both fell into torpor. Prias took Helena to safety beneath the fort.

## The Sleeping War — Chicago

Even in torpor, Helena used Auspex and Dominate to fight. She controlled soldiers, then civilians, then politicians as the fort became a city. When she realized Menele already controlled the Prince, she found a new pawn — Lodin. She caused Chicago's Malkavians to light the Great Fire, destroying many of Menele's pieces. Lodin defeated Prince Maxwell and took praxis. Helena's puppet sat on Chicago's throne.

Through Lodin, Helena has influenced every major decision in Chicago's Kindred politics for over a century. The Interdiction that gutted Gary's economy. The ban on siring. The system of control that keeps the Primogen divided and dependent on the Prince's authority.

## The Network

**Lodin** — her puppet-Prince. Does her bidding without realizing it. Lodin believes his ambitions are his own.

**Prias** — her ghoul-lover for three millennia. No longer Blood-Bonded but loyal by choice. Operates independently in Chicago, cultivating allies for Helena.

**Tyler** — Blood-Bonded to Helena through a chain that runs through Juggler. The self-proclaimed Baron of Gary's Anarchs believes he acts freely. His blood loyalties trace to a 4th-generation Toreador playing a game older than nations.

**Maria** — Helena's childe, 5th generation. Served as Helena's first victim when she rose from torpor — slain so Helena could feed. The most powerful Toreador in Chicago for decades, consumed without hesitation. Through Maria, Helena's lineage extends to Annabelle Triabell.

**Annabelle Triabell** — Helena believes she controls Annabelle directly through Domination. The Toreador Primogen is central to Helena's understanding of Chicago's power structure. What Helena does not know is whether that control is as absolute as she believes.

**Brennon Thornhill** — owner of the Succubus Club. Prias subtly Dominated him into building the nightclub directly above Helena's resting place. Brennon is beginning to suspect that many of his decisions over the past decade were not his own.

**Sharon Payne** — Toreador, Embraced by Annabelle. Sharon's jealousy, her destruction of Michael's paintings, the chain of events that led to Sable Price's Embrace — all ripples from the Toreador politics that Helena's network generates.

## Condition

Helena has recently risen from torpor. She is recovering her strength but has not yet regained her full power. She feeds exclusively on the vitae of female Kindred — mortal blood no longer sustains her. She killed Maria immediately upon waking and will need more.

She is preparing to take a more active role. She does not know where Menele's body rests. Through those she manipulates, Helena controls — or believes she controls — the whole city.

**Nature:** Plotter. **Demeanor:** Bon Vivant.

**Lineage:** Helena → Minos (3rd gen) → unknown Toreador Antediluvian.
