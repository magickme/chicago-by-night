Pieter is a South African exile and anti-apartheid poet who was Embraced in 1975. He is a childe of [Helena Taylor](/npcs/helena-taylor/) and maintains a rebellious, artistic temperament that is often at odds with the Pyramid's rigid hierarchy.

He has Anarch sympathies, though they are tolerated because he is a useful researcher and his dissent is largely seen as academic.
