Embraced 1830. Over fifty confirmed kills on blood hunts. Sits at the right hand of power and is feared by every Kindred in London who has reason to fear anything. Hirsute mountain of a man -- mutton-chop sideburns, meticulous grooming, the kind of physical presence that makes a room contract.

Secretly loathes the current power structure. Has been quietly seeking a conspirator strong enough to depose the prince. The search has gone on long enough that the loathing has curdled into something worse than ambition. Humanity 3.

**Image:** Massive frame, Victorian-era grooming. Sideburns immaculate. Clothes practical but well-kept. Moves like a predator pretending to be furniture.

**Haven:** Near London Zoo. The animals know him.
