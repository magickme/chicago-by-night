Embraced 1880. Childe of [Raphael](/npcs/raphael-catarari/) de Corazon, one of the founders of the Camarilla. Dark hair, blue eyes, the practiced stillness of a man accustomed to courtrooms. The foremost legal mind in London's Kindred society -- his interpretations of the Six Traditions carry the weight of precedent.

Something is changing. He has begun developing a taste for violence against his servants -- mortal and otherwise. The Beast is surfacing in ways his legal mind cannot rationalize, and he is not yet frightened enough to seek help.

**Image:** Dark hair, blue eyes. Barrister's bearing. Impeccable dress. The composure is starting to crack at the edges.
