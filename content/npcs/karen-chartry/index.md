Embraced in Italy in the mid-1700s. Traveled extensively before arriving in New Orleans in the 1940s. Has allied herself with Ventrue in multiple cities and moved on — New Orleans is the longest she's stayed anywhere.

The [Josua Cambridge](/npcs/josua-cambridge/) situation: Karen obsessed over the young art student at the University of New Orleans. When he ignored her, she planned revenge — had Julia pose as a patron to destroy his confidence, then his life. Julia Embraced him instead, panicked and left. Now [Josua](/npcs/josua-cambridge/) lives at [Marcel](/npcs/marcel-guilbeau/)'s, making friends among the Kindred, while Karen presents herself as his ally, steering suspicion toward [Morgaine](/npcs/morgaine/).

Her Humanity is 3. She is very comfortable making people disappear.
