Mrs. Marsh is a sixty-two-year-old widow who has been cleaning the chantry for years. She comes in on Tuesdays and Thursdays. She knows the residents are "private," but she remains blissfully unaware of their true nature. She is kind, grandmotherly, and possesses a sharp memory for the grandchildren whose photos she carries in her purse.

She lives in a small apartment on Devon Avenue and brings in tamales from her favorite local spot, noticing that Tomás enjoys cooking Mexican food for the household.
