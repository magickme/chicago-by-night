Sofía is a second-generation Mexican-American living in El Paso, Texas. For thirty years, she cleaned houses in the Coronado Hills neighborhood, where the Army officers lived. She is a woman of quiet, steel-like dignity who has never let the hardships of the border break her.

Since the death of her husband [Ernesto](/npcs/ernesto-navarro/), she has lived alone in the house on Ochoa Street. She remains a devout Catholic, attending Mass at Sacred Heart every Sunday and lighting a candle for her son, whom the Army reported dead in a "classified training accident" in 1988. She doesn't believe it.
