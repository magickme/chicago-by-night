Runs Milford's Music Hall in Whitechapel -- an original Victorian building offering cabaret and burlesque. Daughter of the original mortal proprietor, Embraced sometime in the early 1900s. The music hall is neutral ground where Kindred of all factions can enjoy entertainment without the formalities of Elysium.

The building is hers. Whitechapel is hers. She has outlasted everything the East End has thrown at her and intends to keep doing so.

**Image:** Ingenue affect that masks a century of survival instincts. Dresses for the stage even when she's not on it.
