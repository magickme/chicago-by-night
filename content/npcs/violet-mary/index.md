Embraced 1854. Always Obfuscated at gatherings. Wears elaborate ballroom gowns and funereal attire that nobody can see -- dresses for an audience of one. Reports transgressions to other harpies and the seneschal. Every Kindred in London knows she is present at every significant event; none can confirm it.

Sleeps in children's graves using Earth Meld. The graves are always Victorian, always small.

**Image:** Unseen. The gowns are real. The beauty is remembered from a life that ended in 1854.
