Mickey served with Tomás at the 470th Military Intelligence Group at Fort Sam Houston, San [Antonio](/npcs/antonio-calbullarshi/). They both worked the Central American desk during the 1980s, mapping the Contra war and the shadow logistics that led to the Iran-Contra scandal.

Unlike Tomás, who chose the commission and the path to the DIA, Mickey remained an NCO — a choice he made specifically to stay close to the data and away from the politics. He is cynical, observant, and possesses a streetwise intelligence that balanced Tomás's clinical analysis.
