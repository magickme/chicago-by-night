Mithras's former seneschal, confidant, and lover. Escaped the Gunpowder Mills explosion by retreating into the Shadowlands. Now operates as "Pater [Thomas](/npcs/thomas-gerhieren/)," managing the largest Mithraic temple from [Doughty House](/locations/doughty-house/) in Richmond.

Knows Mithras survived and sleeps in [Epping Forest](/locations/epping-forest/). Bides his time. Can communicate with the living through wraith abilities. Has been using Dominate through wraith contacts to turn [Valerius](/npcs/valerius/)'s bodyguards loyal to Mithras.

The love outlasted death. The obsession outlasted everything else.

**Image:** Unseen by most. When he manifests, the impression is of a medieval scholar -- gaunt, intense, dressed in robes that haven't been fashionable for centuries.
