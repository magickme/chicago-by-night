Brujah Methuselah. Childe of Troile. 4th generation. Four thousand years of accumulated power compressed into a body that has not moved in centuries. Eastern Mediterranean descent. Wears a simple silver and onyx necklace engraved with Egyptian hieroglyphs — the onyx shaped as a tooth.

## Origin

Meneleus was a scholar-king when Troile found him after the fall of the Second City. Troile had traveled widely since its destruction, and after a night's conversation with the young philosopher, spent months in deep dialogue. Troile decided Menele possessed all the elements worthy of immortality. The Embrace made him 4th generation — one step from the Antediluvians.

Menele was present at Carthage during its glory. The Brujah dream of a city where Kindred and kine lived together as equals. When Rome destroyed Carthage — aided by a Toreador's betrayal — Menele was one of the only 4th-generation Brujah to survive the massacre. The betrayal and the slaughter of his friends became the wound that would not close.

He has pursued Helena across continents for three millennia. Through Egypt, Spain, the New World. He allied with the Aztecs, the Maya, the Inca — all destroyed by Helena's manipulation of the Conquistadors. He fled north through the Pueblos, across the plains. In 1820, on the Kansas prairie, they fought again. At Fort Dearborn, allied with Chief Black Hawk, Menele nearly won. Both Methuselahs dealt each other crippling blows. Both fell into torpor. Their followers carried the war forward.

## The Sleeping War

Torpid beneath Chicago for over a century and a half, but his mind is active. Psychically communes with followers across continents through Auspex and Dominate of extraordinary power — he can Dominate through touch alone, without eye contact. The Bond carries his will to the surface through Critias, Inyanga, and Annabelle — but the communion is more than Bond compulsion. He reaches into their thoughts and leaves instructions that feel like inspiration. The puppet does not feel the strings because the strings are woven from his own desires.

## The Network

**Critias** — his childe, Blood-Bonded. Brujah Primogen of Chicago. The "Brujah School," the Path of Entelechy, the vision of Chicago as the new Carthage — convictions Critias considers his own philosophical legacy. They originate from Menele's sleeping mind.

**Inyanga** — fell under Menele's control shortly after arriving in Chicago. Through her, Menele commands the entire Gangrel clan of the city. If word comes from Inyanga, they all listen, and in very short order every Gangrel can be mobilized. Her Laibon immigration operation through Lucian's docks in Gary moves bodies and resources through Menele's corridor.

**Annabelle Triabell** — the deepest play. Menele sacrificed Maldavis to make Helena believe she had taken control of the Toreador Primogen. Helena thinks continued use of her Domination forced Annabelle to switch sides. In fact, Annabelle is Menele's double agent inside Helena's camp. Through Annabelle, Menele has a window into everything Helena's network does.

**Annabelle → Modius** — Annabelle sired the Prince of Gary. Whether Modius knows his sire serves a Brujah Methuselah is irrelevant. The siring pipeline Modius created — welcoming dumped Chicago neonates to spite Lodin — feeds Menele's reserve. Gary is not exile. Gary is a staging ground.

**Annabelle → Sharon → Michael → Sable** — Annabelle sired Sharon Payne. Sharon's husband Michael was also Embraced through Annabelle. Michael Embraced Sable Price and abandoned her. She drifted to Gary. Four steps from a sleeping god.

**Ublo-Satha** — a sleeper agent inside the Tremere. A Gargoyle conditioned before her transformation. Her dual loyalty is unknown to the Tremere hierarchy. Whatever task Menele has prepared her for, it has not yet been triggered.

**Heath Quinn** — a mortal operative who carried Menele's blood to London in 1969. Shot Roarke on Piccadilly, then fed him a vial of 4th-generation vitae. The twenty-year weapon: Roarke returned to Chicago, lived among the Anarchs, found the torpid Methuselah, and eventually kidnapped Lodin — removing Helena's puppet-Prince from the board.

## Method

Menele does not command. He arranges. Independent actors pursuing their own goals advance his position without knowing it. A Ventrue sire plants a childe in Gary to exploit the docks — the pipeline serves Menele's logistics. A Toreador neonate flees Chicago for Gary — she strengthens a court Menele's network already controls. A hunter destabilizes Gary's Kindred — the pressure weakens Helena's camp.

Tired of fighting Helena. Four millennia of war with a single adversary, and the weariness runs deeper than the hatred. He desires to build an enlightened society of Kindred and kine — something that outlasts the Jyhad, something Troile would have recognized as worthy. He sought Golconda once, and was interrupted. The dream persists.

"Menele will wake soon, and everything must be ready."
