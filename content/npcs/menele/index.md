Brujah Methuselah. Childe of Troile. 4th generation. Four thousand years of accumulated power compressed into a body that has not moved in centuries.

Torpid beneath Chicago, but his mind is active. Psychically communes with followers across continents. The Bond carries his will to the surface through Critias, Inyanga, and Annabelle — but the communion is more than Bond compulsion. He reaches into their thoughts and leaves instructions that feel like inspiration.

Subtly influencing Critias without his childe's knowledge. The "Brujah School," the Path of Entelechy, the vision of Chicago as the new Carthage — these convictions that Critias considers his own philosophical legacy originate from Menele's sleeping mind. The puppet does not feel the strings because the strings are woven from his own desires.

Has a sleeper agent inside the Tremere: Ublo-Satha, a Gargoyle conditioned before her transformation. Her dual loyalty is unknown to the Tremere hierarchy. Whatever task Menele has prepared her for, it has not yet been triggered.

Sponsors the Path of Entelechy worldwide. Reaching out to followers in Greece and Turkey through the psychic communion, guiding candidates toward philosophy teachers, cultivating a generation of Brujah thinkers who will be waiting when he rises.

Tired of fighting Helena. Four millennia of war with a single adversary, and the weariness runs deeper than the hatred. He desires to build an enlightened society of Kindred and kine — something that outlasts the Jyhad, something Troile would have recognized as worthy.

"Menele will wake soon, and everything must be ready."

**Lineage:** Menele > Troile (Antediluvian) > Brujah (Clan Founder).
