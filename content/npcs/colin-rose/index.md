Embraced in 1974, Colin is a socialite and pianist who manages the chantry's public image and social connections in McLean, Virginia. He is the childe of [Helena Taylor](/npcs/helena-taylor/) and serves as a PR man for the Pyramid.

Unlike many of his fellow apprentices, Colin is more concerned with his social standing and his music than with the deeper occult research or political maneuvering of the DC Chantry.
