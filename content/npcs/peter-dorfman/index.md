Dorfman is one of the most politically powerful Tremere in North America. He oversees the Pyramid's influence within the US government, controlling numerous PACs and having ghouls in several agencies. He is a tall, sharp-featured man who wears Armani suits made of Teflon and carries a walking stick enchanted with protective wards.

He is ambitious, but his focus on the internal politics of the Pyramid — especially his rivalry with Pontifex Powell — sometimes leads him to overlook more immediate threats in his own chantry.
