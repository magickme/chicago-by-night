Keeper of Elysium. Was involved with the Mithraic Mysteries in the Victorian era -- an entanglement that left her terrified of Mithras and everything connected to his cult. Her childe [Rose](/npcs/colin-rose/) Abawi is susceptible to Mithras's influence, a vulnerability Regina monitors with quiet dread.

Manages London's major Elysia with precision and taste. The South Bank arts complex is her domain and her armor.

**Image:** Composed, cultured, the kind of presence that makes a gallery opening feel curated even when it isn't. The fear lives underneath.
