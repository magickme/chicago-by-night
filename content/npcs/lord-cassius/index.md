Cassius is a veteran of the Pyramid's long-term infiltration of mortal military and intelligence structures. He operates as a "talent scout" and handler, identifying gifted mortals within the DIA, CIA, and NSA who possess the specific blend of analytical preternaturalism and institutional loyalty the Tremere prize.

He is a tall, imposing man with an accent that betrays a Central European origin, though his mortal paper trail places him as a retired Army colonel from Virginia. He is clinical, strategic, and views his childer more as intelligence assets than as family.
