Embraced 1576. Runs a working asylum with a secret underground wing -- the Deep Ward -- where Kindred are interrogated, broken, and sometimes kept indefinitely. His own sire is blood-bound and imprisoned as a patient in the Deep Ward, a relationship Timothy maintains with clinical detachment and private satisfaction.

Cooperates with the Ventrue on "disappearing" political enemies. The arrangement is mutually beneficial: the Ventrue get deniability, Timothy gets subjects. Possesses the Iron Will merit, making him functionally immune to Dominate -- a fact he keeps quiet.

**Image:** Victorian physician's affect. Calm, measured speech. The kind of doctor who asks how you're feeling while his orderlies strap you down.
