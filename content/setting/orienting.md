## Notes on Other Materials

Over its lifetime, *Vampire: The Masquerade* has gone through nearly a dozen different editions and settings, all of which are radically different from each other—particularly the most recent version, *V5*, which has very little to do with the game described above.

Because of this, new *Vampire* players can easily become overwhelmed with information (over a hundred sourcebooks have also been published for the game over the years).

In order to make this game as enjoyable as possible, here are my suggestions:

- **Don't read any of the published material on the game**, other than what I send you. I will keep this to the bare minimum, and provide you with any necessary material in PDF form, to keep it simple.

- **Don't spend time researching the game online or on YouTube.** You're just going to get a bunch of spoilers that ruin the fun and conflicting information that will confuse you. That said, there are two very well produced YouTube series of professional actors playing the game—[Los Angeles by Night](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7atuZxmT957CplbNHCN5JAGp9SoZhyUH) and [New York by Night](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL4qzoETfmhLZDmpmGP_rs4bgstBFZmlzN)—that are truly excellent for getting the feel for the game as it is actually played. They do contain spoilers, so tread lightly; however, my version of the Vampire "universe" is likely going to be very, very different from the published material.

- **Definitely do NOT read any of the published material about Chicago, Milwaukee, Washington D.C., or any "By Night" book** for that matter. You will ruin the game for yourself, and it will unfortunately be considered cheating.

- **For those who may already have familiarity with the game**—in my version, there is no metaplot. It never existed, and is effectively blank-slated.

## Getting Better at the Game

<img src="/setting-images/archon.webp" alt="Camarilla enforcer" class="w-full rounded-lg my-6" />
<figcaption>Camarilla enforcer hunts and kills Anarch insurgents under the cover of US peacekeeping operations in Sarajevo, 1992.</figcaption>

While *Vampire* is not a game you can "win," you may at some point wonder how you can "get better" at the game (reading other *Vampire* books isn't the answer). Because this is a game primarily about power and politics, my recommendation would be to consume movies, TV shows and books in that vein, and consider how your character and Coterie might use some of the strategies you find.

Examples would be:
- The show *House of Cards*
- Machiavelli's *The Prince*
- Sun Tzu's *Art of War*
- Robert Greene's *48 Laws of Power* or *Art of Seduction*
- Many others

Any time spent with this material should also prove very beneficial to your "real" life which, after all, is the point of this game. Any time spent practicing improvisational acting as your character, either by yourself or with partners, will of course be vastly rewarding and satisfying as well.
