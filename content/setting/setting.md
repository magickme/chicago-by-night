## Gary, Indiana: Sick City

Gary sits directly **southeast of Chicago** at the bottom of Lake Michigan. Once America's steel manufacturing capital, by 1990 it is a rusted skeleton—economically obliterated by Reagan's union-busting, corporate plundering, and global outsourcing.

**The Blight**: Gary appears less like a once-thriving American city and more like a bombed-out European city after World War II. Gangs, drug dealers, organized crime, human traffickers, and vampires chew the corpse of what was once the American Dream of union jobs and stable employment.

### Why It Matters to the Kindred

Unlike jeweled Chicago—the Camarilla's shining city ruled by **Prince Lodin** (Ventrue)—Gary is a **lawless no-man's-land**.

For the Kindred, this means:

1. **Free Hunting**: You can hunt and feed without encroaching on a more powerful vampire's territory.
2. **Free Breeding**: You can embrace new vampires without Lodin's permission. In Chicago, only the elite may create new Kindred after decades of service at the Prince's blessing. Gary has no such restriction.
3. **Anarch Territory**: The lawlessness attracts revolutionary Kindred who refuse Camarilla rule.

### The Political Destruction

In the 1980s, **Prince Lodin** deliberately orchestrated Gary's economic collapse to destroy his rival, **Modius** (Toreador Prince of Gary).

Modius's ego had long chafed under Lodin's shadow. For a century, he'd tried to claw away some of Lodin's spotlight. Like Salieri to Mozart, he was simply no match for Lodin's superior political acumen, mastery of corporate power, and favored status within the Sect.

**Lodin's Strategy**: By maneuvering corporate institutions and the Republican Party to break American union power and collapse the steel industry—Gary's entire power base—Lodin devastated Modius without direct confrontation.

**The Cost**: Hundreds of thousands of mortals lost jobs, families, lives to suicide. The unemployment, despair, and abandonment of Gary would ripple across America for decades, leaving much of the country as blighted as Gary, eventually addicted to Oxycontin.

**The Result**: Modius is broken. Gary is abandoned by the Camarilla establishment. But this abandonment is Gary's advantage: it has become a free hunting and breeding ground.

### The Millennium Approaches

It is within this anarchic wilderness of rusted steel that our characters find themselves in late 1990. The American Dream lies dead in the streets of Gary. The Millennium fast approaches, bringing with it an apocalyptic tide of cults, spiritualism, decadence and violence.

Beneath the surface, the Kindred play their ancient games, like spiders and beetles in the undergrowth, manipulating the new world order from the shadows to conform to their own dark appetites.
