Two weeks ago, you found the following handwritten letter inside a large brown envelope in your mailbox, along with over 275 pages of photocopied material from what appear to be publisher's galleys of a book.

---

Hey, check this out—I was just in Chicago and this Nosferatu lick I met gave me this. He said a couple months ago the Camarilla busted a Malkavian down in Atlanta who was trying to put out information about us in the form of—get this—some kind of game. Apparently the Malk in question thought he was actually *upholding* the Masquerade by Dominating mortals into putting out fictional stories about us that make it seem like mortals are schizos who are reciting stuff from games and comics and movies and thinking it's real.

Well, the Camarilla didn't think so, because a bunch of the stuff in this book seems pretty spot on to me, even though it reads like the Sunday funnies most of the time. Looks like they redacted a whole bunch of stuff too, so who knows what this crazy Malk slipped in there. Nobody's heard from him since. You do the math.

Funny thing is, apparently one of the Nos anarchs got hold of a redacted copy and he's been showing it around to all the kids. I actually thought it was pretty useful. Helped me think about a few things in a bigger picture kinda way.

Of course, most of this is bullshit—I mean, whoever wrote this seems to think that there are 13 clans instead of 7 (??? Do they mean, like, a couple gangs of hapless Caitiff losers?), claimed there were sects beside the Camarilla (we should be so lucky!! The anarchs are hopeless enough as it is), that Caine was the first vampire (please!!!! Sunday school is back that way), and all kinds of other silliness.

Hopefully you'll get a kick out of it. Course, you didn't get this from me. Burn it after you read it or bury it in your back yard or something. It's a joke, but we all know how these Camarilla stiffs can take a joke.

Hope to see you soon.

Evelyn

P.S. I got to wondering—why did they redact it? Only Camarilla enforcers were supposed to have it. Maybe there were things in there somebody didn't even want the rest of the Fang Patrol to see?
