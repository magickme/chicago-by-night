## Acts I and II


### Act I: Gary, December 31, 1990 through December 31, 1991

Darius entered Gary as Chuc Luc's concealed instrument, using Warren Birch's invented lineage while building a waterfront channel and a mortal business apparatus. Sable entered a small court where haven, patronage and personal freedom were all bargains with people who expected obedience. Their eventual alliance made both less dependent on any one patron, while tying them to each other's secrets.

Blood at Dawn, the Torch auction and the struggle around the club resolved. Darius established a division of business with Juggler and Victor, acquired the Torch mortgage obligation, negotiated dock access with Lucian and installed Webb as the Warren Birch proxy. The business machinery survived the move to Chicago. Its upkeep and exposure survived too.

Sable killed Big Six, dismantled Williams's slave auction and neutralized Spoon's immediate threat. Her relationship with Allicia developed into an escape project with real blood ties and a cost to both women. Allicia's dependence on Modius was never a problem that could be solved by one rescue scene. Sable's ghoul network initially gave her reach; its later dissolution and reconstruction in Chicago changed what that reach could sustain.

Darius and Sable formed their coterie on Jul 21, 1991. By Baptism by Fire on New Year's Eve, they had holdings, contacts, enemies and substantial secrets. Modius sent them to Chicago as emissaries. They left Gary with a working apparatus behind them and several people who still expected service.


### Act II: Chicago, January 1 through June 13, 1992

The emissary errand became Ashes to Ashes. The coterie rescued Lodin at Hell's Pasture, destroyed Roarke and delivered the torpored elder later identified as Menele into Lodin's custody. Darius drank from the elder's vial in frenzy. The rescue gave Darius and Sable a major outstanding claim on the Prince; later documents disagree on its exact grade, but no scene spends or cancels it.

The coterie then learned to act inside Chicago's court. Annabelle's Party and Drummond's testimony exposed Ballard. The Primogen reprimanded him; the later counterattack was defeated and the case closed. Ballard survived with his institutions and his grudge. In Player of Pawns, Darius helped Critias hold Dimitri, whom Critias diablerized. Critias's freely offered Major boon was banked. Fundamental Differences ended with Coleman alive and transported to Gary, Brennon's anger redirected toward Modius, and a stronger coterie channel into the Prince's office.

Tomás joined the formal three-person pact at D047/S046 on March 5. Lodin acknowledged Darius's real lineage, recognized the coterie's domain and made them useful court operatives. Warren Birch stopped protecting Darius's identity in Chicago. Tomás's simultaneous duty to the Pyramid became a recurring conflict with the pact's disclosure and consensus requirements.

The tunnel investigations exposed several overlapping operations without solving all of them. Paper Chase neutralized the immediate hunter trap and mapped the Society of Leopold's network; it left the network alive and compromised. Grand Elusion ended with Ehrich in Tremere custody, Gibson extracted and Orloff detained. Ehrich supplied a bond-breaking lead, rather than a reliable cure the coterie could simply administer.

Blood Bond began at the April 4 opera. Foster was freed, Bach's pack fought publicly, and Lodin forced one drink of his blood on all three PCs afterward. Neally's collapse brought public feeding, the orphanage horror, Becky's delivery to Lodin and Lorraine's rescue. The coterie identified Foster and Emily Carter before the published reveal. Lodin declared the Hunt and secretly commissioned the coterie to recover a functional, unbound Neally.

During that commission, Sable extracted Allicia from Gary and delivered her to Carna in Milwaukee. The extraction is complete. The experimental treatment has only begun. Darius restored contact with Chuc Luc and accepted further work in Chinatown. The pact adapted to controlled disclosure after the Allicia operation, without resolving Tomás's divided obligations.

The Ride, the cemetery hearing and the agreed cellar rite culminated in D069. Nicolai verified that Foster's old bond over Neally was gone. Darius used his last Willpower point to obtain Lodin's signed discharge for all three PCs and revocation of Neally's existing Hunt. Before Maryann, the coterie gave an account of the deniable mission. Neally declared himself Sabbat and chose voluntary Archon custody. Bach heard that choice and accepted the account. Darius's conditional Denver introduction remains owed.

Dane attacked the Drake departure. Darius fired four rounds, inflicted two lethal damage and punctured a rear tire. Dane escaped alive. All three PCs withdrew unhurt after recovering all four spent casings. The borrowed van remained damaged, gasoline-soaked and unlit, with its keys left beside it. The final question in Maryann's office concerns the previous night's feeding.

Lodin is alive and remains Prince. He retains the police, media and government apparatus that made him dangerous before the rescue. The coterie has recognized standing, a granted domain and a signed record that it completed this commission. The three PCs remain associated with the Camarilla and each retains the established first step of a blood tie to Lodin.

Lodin wanted usable Edwards back and evidence that bonds could be broken, which he intends to use in an argument before the Primogen for imposing more bonds. That motive has been revealed. The council has not yet accepted his argument. Maryann now has the witness, Nicolai's narrow finding and testimony about Lodin's deniable instructions. Masquerade Heat is 6/6. Maryann's arrival is the consequence already played at that cap. Andrzej's death, Dee's hospitalization and the Drake gunfire remain distinct evidence risks. A recovered casing does not remove bullets, witnesses or property damage.

### Camarilla containment pressure

A declared Sabbat inside the Prince's administration makes a wider security response likely. Maryann has reason to secure testimony and trace access, blood couriers and compromised contacts; Lodin has reason to demonstrate control, and the Primogen will contest his emergency powers. These are the expected next moves. No new purge, universal Hunt or completed security audit has been played. The coterie's signed success stands while its witness obligations become more consequential.

Milwaukee remains ahead as a regional story. Delivering Allicia to Carna did not complete Milwaukee by Night or Psychomachia. The coming Sabbat perspective can develop alongside this tightening Camarilla posture without forcing the later Chicago war to begin now.

### The Sabbat bargain leaves a channel open

Bach's pack fulfilled the rite bargain. Foster's old bond over Neally is verified broken. Neally has openly declared his membership, his old Hunt has been withdrawn, and he remains with Maryann by choice. Actual departure of Bach's pack is unplayed.

Neally still possesses his knowledge of Chicago's administration. Voluntary Archon custody prevents his immediate departure with the pack in the played ending; it does not erase that knowledge. What he has already told Foster, and what he may later disclose, have not been audited in play. His survival and declared allegiance therefore remain a security concern even though the commission is complete.

A separate infiltration remains unresolved (GM details in the continuity appendix). Settling with Bach does not clear Chicago of Sabbat activity. The Denver undertaking may connect the next arc to Chicago, but no Denver name, direct contact or guaranteed cure has been handed over. No PC has joined the Sabbat, acquired an initiation medallion or begun Under a Blood Red Moon.

### Gary and Milwaukee remain consequential

Modius's Internal Audit is 5/6. Allicia is absent; his response has not been played. His sealed envelope waits with Walt's landlady, unopened. Darius's Gary obligations and Sable's former service arrangements were not dissolved by a Chicago discharge.

Allicia is at Carna's protected Milwaukee location. Weekly treatment is experimental. Sable's anchor visit is due around June 16, arranged through Erichtho. No cure or completed liberation is recorded. Leaving the Chicago PCs offscreen requires an explicit way to preserve that care.
