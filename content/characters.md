## Player Characters

**[Darius Cole](/darius-cole/)** — Ventrue, 10th generation. Street broker from Midtown Gary. Planted in the city by a sire nobody knows exists, building a money pipeline through an ancient Gangrel's docks. Feeds on people who owe debts they can't pay.

**[Sable Price](/sable-price/)** — Toreador, 9th generation. Exotic dancer from the Robert Taylor Homes. Abandoned childe of a painter at war with his ex-wife. Fled Chicago for Gary with Presence, a stolen fur coat, and nothing else.
