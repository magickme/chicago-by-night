## Function in Play

Where New Orleans Kindred go when they want to be among their own kind without the formal weight of Elysium. [Jackson Square and the Cabildo](/locations/jackson-square-and-the-cabildo/) are for politics; the Twilight Club is for everything else. Rumors, deals, casual feeding, and the informal hierarchies that form when Kindred are off the record.

Not a fixed address — the club relocates every few months through a rotating series of French Quarter venues that [Marcel](/npcs/marcel-guilbeau/) or his people have arrangements with. Regular Kindred know the current location. Newcomers have to ask around, which serves its own filtering function.

## Physical Read

Whatever venue it occupies: dark, late-night hours only, music loud enough to cover conversation. Mixed mortal and Kindred attendance without hard separation. The mortals who come are regulars who have learned not to look too closely. The Kindred who come have learned the same.
