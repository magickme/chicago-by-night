## Function in Play

The operational base for the Followers of Set in New Orleans. [Marcel](/npcs/marcel-guilbeau/) has outlawed Setites; this location is the hidden center of their presence. The hotel provides legitimate business cover and a fixed temple space — the opposite of the mobile Dock operation.

## Physical Read

A once-popular resort on Lake Pontchartrain, now visibly run-down. The exterior says "struggling business." The lobby says "don't stay here" — Setite retainers lounge around and the verbal abuse directed at any mortal seeking a room is usually enough to clear them out. Behind the lobby, the hotel's back rooms are well-maintained: the temple space, storage for smuggling operations, meeting rooms.

## Who Controls It

[Manon](/npcs/manon/), through the hotel owner (a Retainer to the Setite leadership). An "unknown benefactor" saved the hotel from closing — this benefactor is the Setite network.
