## Function in Play

- The London Chapter House of the Arcanum — a mortal society of scholars investigating the supernatural.
- A Georgian townhouse handling publications (Annual Proceedings), business operations, and hosting local investigative lodges.
- Jermyn Talbot recently stepped down as London Chancellor (served 1958-1968).
- Active lodges investigate supernatural phenomena across London.
- In 1969, the Arcanum is in its "observe, don't interfere" tradition — but the 1960s occult revival is generating more leads than ever.
- The Foundation House (Vannever Hall) is 100 miles north in the Cambridgeshire Fens.

## Physical Read

- An elegant Georgian townhouse that could belong to any wealthy academic.
- Inside: card catalogues, leather-bound journals, a library that smells of old paper and tobacco.
- A basement filing room with locked cabinets containing dossiers on "unexplained phenomena" that would make a Kindred's blood run cold.
