## Function in Play

The most distinctive nightlife venue for Kindred in New Orleans. On public nights it operates as a standard strip club. On certain Kindred-only nights, the mortal performers — willing "vampire whores" who submit to the Kiss for the euphoria — provide a curated feeding experience that draws Kindred from across the city.

## Physical Read

A small Bourbon Street strip club, nothing from outside to distinguish it. Inside: low lighting, tables close together, a performance area. On mortal nights it's indistinguishable from a hundred similar venues. On Kindred nights, the doors lock from inside, the clientele changes, and something different plays.

## Who Controls It

Morgaine owns and manages it. She lives in [the Garden District](/locations/the-garden-district/) but uses this as her primary public presence.
