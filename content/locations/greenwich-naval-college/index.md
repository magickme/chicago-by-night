## Function in Play

- The Old Royal Naval College's Painted Hall serves as the primary Ventrue clan meeting place.
- Clan meetings have been held here regularly since the building's completion.
- The hall's ceiling — one of the largest painted ceilings in Europe — depicts the triumph of British maritime power. The Ventrue find this appropriate.
- In 1969, the building still serves as the Royal Naval College; Ventrue meetings take place after hours, with ghouls among the staff ensuring privacy.

## Physical Read

- Baroque grandeur on the Thames at Greenwich.
- The Painted Hall: 40,000 square feet of allegorical ceiling painting by James [Thornhill](/npcs/brennon-thornhill/). Long tables, candlelight, the smell of old wood and older blood.
