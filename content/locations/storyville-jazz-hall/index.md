## Function in Play

One of the most frequented Kindred social spaces in New Orleans, specifically because it blends traditional jazz with late-night R&B and draws Toreador, Tremere, and Ventrue in a context where the music provides excuse for extended presence. Elysium protection makes it relatively safe for mixed-clan socializing.

## Physical Read

An old building on Decatur, near the river-facing edge of [the French Quarter](/locations/the-french-quarter/). Exposed brick walls, jazz combos on stage most nights, dim lighting. The audience is mixed mortal/Kindred without anyone being entirely certain which is which — which is part of the appeal.
