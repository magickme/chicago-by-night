## Function in Play

- Mithras sleeps beneath the earth here, having fled in frenzy from the Gunpowder Mills explosion in January 1940.
- [Roger de Camden](/npcs/roger-de-camden/) (operating from the Shadowlands) knows Mithras survived and is searching for the exact location.
- The forest is lupine territory — Fianna and Bone Gnawers patrol it. Any Kindred entering risks detection and destruction.
- Centre Point (the famous empty skyscraper at Oxford Street/Tottenham Court Road) is the publicly believed torpor site from earlier canon — in V5 canon, Epping Forest is the truth.

## Physical Read

- 6,000 acres of ancient woodland on London's northeastern fringe. Beech, oak, hornbeam. Iron Age earthworks. Darkness comes early under the canopy.
- The ground here feels wrong — a faint, sub-audible pressure that Auspex registers as something vast and ancient sleeping below.
