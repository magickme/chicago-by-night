*New Orleans, Louisiana / October 19, 1991*

---

Calvi's shadow held the blond man across the room. Another arm of darkness was wrapped around Calvi, holding him fast. Luc lay on the floor between them. The swordsman pulled against his bonds and Calvi tried to catch his eyes, but the man kept his face turned away.

The arm around Calvi tightened. He felt his body give beneath it, and his own shadow squeezed in answer. The swordsman grunted and fought to free himself. Calvi tried to wrench the arm off, but his hands no longer had the strength. Another squeeze left him hanging in its grip.

Constanzo was still at the other end of the fire tunnel. She went back toward the yard, found the service hatch, and lowered herself into the room behind the wall slots. The two damaged flamethrowers remained where they had been left, their fuel lines disconnected. Through one of the slots she saw a silhouette pass in the tunnel. She could make out a pale hand and dark clothing, but no face.

She searched behind the equipment and found a separate pipe disappearing into the masonry. There was a handwheel on it. She tried to turn it and the metal screamed under her grip. The wheel stayed where it was. Beyond the slot, the outline turned toward the sound.

"Who's there?" she asked.

"Who are you?"

The person stayed out of clear view. Constanzo drew back until she was hidden, then returned to the wheel. She put her strength into it. This time it moved, carrying the sound into the tunnel. The pale hand withdrew from the opening. Nothing else visibly changed.

She went back to the tunnel and tried to cross. Fire burst across the far end before she could reach it. She returned to the service room and searched again for a control, then called through the slot, trying to draw the person toward the flames. A voice called back from the dark. She kept it talking until fire flashed through the opening. The sight sent her scrambling for the hatch. Behind the brickwork she stopped, waited, and regained control of herself.

Constanzo returned the wheel to its former position. At the tunnel the fire came again. She looked through the near chambers for another way around and found none she could use. Finally she gave her blood to the muscles in her legs, waited behind the wall while the fire burned, and crossed when the sound of it fell away.

The steel door Luc had forced was open. From its edge she saw a man standing near someone on the floor. She watched until she recognized Calvi. A dark arm tightened around him. The man beside him was short and blond, with a sword in his hand.

She spent more blood, making herself stronger and harder to hurt, while Calvi lay in the grip of the shadow. Then she came out of hiding and caught the swordsman around the body. He tore free before she could sink her teeth into him.

Constanzo tried to hold his gaze. He turned away and struck her. She gathered herself and tried again, and his sword found her a second time. The shadow kept its hold on Calvi throughout. Constanzo reached for the man once more; he cut her down before she got to him.

On the floor she spent blood to heal and tried to rise. The next blow drove her down again. Calvi was out of reach. She had wanted to take his blood before leaving, but she could not get to him while the swordsman stood over them.

She spent another point and tried to move. The man struck before she reached the door. Constanzo fell beside the passage she had crossed alone, and the room went still around her.

The pack had come to New Orleans to find Carl Wisor. Puente was dead in the fire tunnel. Luc lay dead in the room. Calvi and Constanzo were down, and the man with the sword was still standing. No one had taken a captive back to Sangris or brought him proof of Wisor's destruction.
