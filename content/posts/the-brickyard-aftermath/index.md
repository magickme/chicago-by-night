The first commission Sangris gave Les Fossoyeurs ended in a brickyard outside New Orleans. Puente died in the fire tunnel; Luc, the Dancer hired to help the pack, died in the room beyond it. Calvi and Constanzo were left in torpor. Their subsequent fate is unknown. No one in the pack brought Sangris a prisoner or proof that Carl Wisor had been destroyed.

The June 1992 homecoming in which the three packmates returned to Montreal and Constanzo recounted the assignment to Ezekiel has been removed from this continuity. The commission itself still happened. What Sangris or Ezekiel later learns about it, and how, remains to be played or established.

In New Orleans, Dutch and Jake had already been killed, and Constanzo diablerized Tully Jones after the pack seized him. A witness brought police to the scene of Tully's interrogation. Those events give local authorities and Kindred reasons to investigate, but they do not establish that anyone found the brickyard or learned exactly what happened there. The pack's staked prisoner and belongings were last left at a separate refuge; their later fate is unknown.

In Montreal, Sangris's office may eventually ask why the pack did not return. The pack's absence also leaves any claim to Chinatown open. Neither a particular inquiry nor a new claimant has been established. Montreal's independent events continue on their own chronology.

Chicago has no established report connecting its characters to the brickyard. Their existing situation remains as played. A future connection would need someone who knows the relevant facts to carry them there.
