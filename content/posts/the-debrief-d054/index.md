*Kaspar & Sons / [Prudential Building](/locations/prudential-building/) / [Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/) / [University of Chicago Faculty Club](/locations/faculty-club/) / [Gengis's Brewery](/locations/the-brewery/) / [Astor Street Chantry](/locations/tremere-chantry/)*

*Chicago, Illinois*

---

[Darius](/darius-cole/) woke at five fifteen with the day's last light bleeding out of the basement at Kaspar & Sons. Nine units. He'd budgeted ten last night and the night had taken one anyway. He sat up, found his shoes, did the arithmetic before the prayer.

[Sable](/sable-price/) was at the workbench upstairs, gloves on, working a flat-blade screwdriver into the housing of a cassette recorder. [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) stood near the small window with his back to it. The shoulder rig was empty. He'd left the .45 in the chantry safe.

"He's going to ask for the toast," [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) said.

"He's going to ask for the toast," [Darius](/darius-cole/) said.

[Sable](/sable-price/) set the screwdriver down. "Then we walk in already having answered him."

---

*[Prudential Building](/locations/prudential-building/), the Loop. Seven o'clock.*

The elevator doors opened on a hallway that smelled of furniture polish and old paper. [Neally Edwards](/npcs/neally-edwards/) stood at the far end with a clipboard against her thigh, brown wool suit, the same flat mouth she wore at every court appearance. She inclined her head to [Darius](/darius-cole/). To [Sable](/sable-price/). To [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/). The order was the order [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) set.

"His Grace will see you now."

The office took up the north corner of the forty-first floor. Lake on two sides, the city stitched in lights below. [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) sat behind a desk older than [Darius](/darius-cole/)'s grandfather and built to outlast every grandchild he would never have. The Prince did not rise. The room rose around him.

"Coterie." A single word. Enough.

[Darius](/darius-cole/) crossed to the chair on the right. [Sable](/sable-price/) to the left. [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) stood. The hierarchy was the hierarchy.

"You have brought me a report."

[Darius](/darius-cole/) gave it clean. Debrief cadence. Three minutes, no qualifications. Society of Leopold Chicago cell — four operatives, named, located, mapped. Three national distribution points. [Dane](/npcs/dane/)'s pipeline exposed, quarterly bulletins routed through the Society's own classification system, the word *independent-friendly* lifted from his paperwork and used against him. Both field operatives neutralized. Counter-pressure initiated through the Society's bureaucracy. Herdon retained. Sabbat presence in [Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/) confirmed — skull, marker, Serpent of the Light cipher.

[Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) listened. His hands rested on the desk in a way that was not relaxation. The hands of a man who could move them, or not, and either decision changed a city.

When [Darius](/darius-cole/) finished, the Prince looked at [Sable](/sable-price/). Then at [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/). Then back at [Darius](/darius-cole/).

"The boon you hold from [Hell's Pasture](/locations/hells-pasture/) has been earned a second time. That is a debt I do not intend to acknowledge in a way that complicates my city."

A pause that meant nothing because [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) did not perform pauses. He simply spoke when he chose.

"There is a courtesy by which I confirm the loyalty of those who serve me. You know what it is."

[Neally](/npcs/neally-edwards/) had not moved from the wall. The decanter on the side table had been set out before they arrived. Cut crystal, dark glass, the contents the color of a bruise that had not yet finished coming up. [Darius](/darius-cole/) did not look at it. He had been looking at it since he sat down.

"Your Grace." He kept his hands on his thighs. Open palms. Visible. "I understand what you're offering. And I understand what it means."

He held the Prince's eye. Not a challenge. A measurement.

"It means you will never be sure if my loyalty is real or bought. That is a serious thing to put into a man like you, and a serious thing to take in. You are within your rights to order us. We won't say otherwise if you do." He drew the next sentence out of a place he'd been keeping it since [Hell's Pasture](/locations/hells-pasture/). "All I ask is one month. Give us a chance — give me a chance — to prove the loyalty is already there. If you're not satisfied at the end of it, we will take the cup. But I am of your line. I would rather earn the place than have it poured into me."

He felt the words leave and did not call them back.

The clock on the wall measured what came next. Five seconds. Seven. [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/)'s expression did not change. [Sable](/sable-price/)'s hand moved a quarter inch on the arm of her chair and stopped. [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) breathed once, evenly, through his nose.

[Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) spoke to [Neally](/npcs/neally-edwards/) without turning his head.

"Weekly reports to your office. Hand-delivered. Sabbat and hunter intelligence routed direct, no delay. Written assessment of the Sabbat presence on my desk by Monday." Then back to [Darius](/darius-cole/). "Three days. If the assessment is what it should be, the month begins on its delivery. If the month is what it should be, we revisit this conversation. If it is not — "

The Prince's hand moved at last. A small turn of the wrist toward the decanter.

" — there will not be a second courtesy."

"Understood, Your Grace."

"Your coterie."

"They go with their leader, Your Grace," [Sable](/sable-price/) said. Her voice carried the part of itself she used in court: silk over steel cable. "And we are agreed on what the leader said."

[Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) inclined his head a fraction. Said nothing. Said everything. Tremere posture. Chain of command read in degrees of spinal angle.

[Lodin](/npcs/lodin/) held them in his attention a moment longer, the way a man holds a chess piece before placing it. Then he set them down.

"Dismissed."

---

The elevator carried the three of them back down through the building in silence. The numbers ticked. Forty. Thirty-five. [Sable](/sable-price/) studied her own reflection in the brass paneling and did not blink. [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) had gone somewhere internal, the place where he wrote his after-action notes before he wrote them.

At the lobby [Darius](/darius-cole/) said, "You take the car. I'm going south."

[Sable](/sable-price/) looked at him. "[Hyde Park](/locations/hyde-park/)?"

"After Pilsen. I want to walk a block of it first."

She did not ask. She put her gloved hand briefly on his sleeve, not a squeeze, just contact, and walked out with [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) toward the parking garage. [Darius](/darius-cole/) cut east on Randolph for the CTA.

---

*[Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/), eight forty-five. Eighteenth and Loomis.*

The bus put him down in a stretch of street where the streetlamps were two out of three and the third was the wrong color. February air had the chemical taste it took on south of the river — diesel, fryer grease, the cold metal of the El. He walked west with his collar up.

The man on the corner was not waiting for him. The man on the corner was waiting for someone else. Forty-five. Tan jacket too thin for the weather, the build of a man who had been muscle in 1975 and was now weight worn the right way. A face [Darius](/darius-cole/) did not know.

Their eyes met across the intersection. The man recognized a category. The way [Darius](/darius-cole/)'s coat hung. The way his hands were not in his pockets. He took a step forward.

"You're him." He said it without aggression. The way a man says a thing he has been carrying for weeks. "You're the one they been talking about."

Brad Hessler. The name came up later, from a wallet. For now the man was the situation. A folding knife pulling the right pocket of the jacket out of plumb. The sobriety that grief gave a man. A daughter somewhere or a brother or a friend who had ended up in a basement with marks on her neck.

[Darius](/darius-cole/) let his hands stay open. Took one step closer. The streetlamp threw their shadows long behind them.

"Look at me."

Hessler looked. The pupils widened, the way they did. [Darius](/darius-cole/) found the place behind the eyes where the man's grief lived and slid his words in alongside it, gently, the way you slip a key into a lock that has not been oiled in years.

"There is a man. You know his face. You know what he did. Tonight you are going to find him, and you are going to settle it. When it's done, you will not remember how. You will only know that the thing that was eating you is gone. Your sense of justice will be answered. You will live a good life after. You will be a man at peace. Go."

Hessler stood for the space of one breath. His face did not change in any way the street would have read. Then he turned and walked east, the way [Darius](/darius-cole/) had come, his hand already in the pocket with the knife.

[Darius](/darius-cole/) stood in the wedge of bad light at the corner and watched the man's back recede.

His Beast moved once, low in his chest, the way it did when a useful tool had been used. Not hunger. Not satisfaction. Acknowledgment. *That was clean.* He pushed it down. The man Hessler was going to kill was a man Hessler believed deserved it. Maybe he did. Maybe he didn't. That part wasn't [Darius](/darius-cole/)'s anymore. He had bought himself a quiet hour in [Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/) and a man's worth of conscience to carry afterward, and the Beast was not the part of him doing the carrying.

He turned south toward Hyde Park and the faculty club.

---

*[University of Chicago Faculty Club](/locations/faculty-club/), Hyde Park. Nine o'clock.*

The Faculty Club at this hour was wood smoke and Bordeaux, low lamps in green shades, a fire built down to coals. [Critias](/npcs/critias/) was at his usual table, back to the wall, sightline to both doors. A book of Greek lay closed beside his glass.

[Critias](/npcs/critias/) did not stand when [Darius](/darius-cole/) approached. He gestured to the chair opposite.

"Mr. Cole. You look like a man who has had an evening."

[Darius](/darius-cole/) sat. "I'm starting to enjoy Chicago, I have to say."

A beat. [Critias](/npcs/critias/)'s mouth moved at one corner. The smile of a man who recognized when someone had given him a line worth keeping.

"That is a more dangerous statement than you may intend."

"It's intended exactly."

A waiter passed without slowing. [Critias](/npcs/critias/) did not look at him. The waiter did not look at [Critias](/npcs/critias/). There was a small envelope on the table between them that [Darius](/darius-cole/) had not noticed when he sat down. [Critias](/npcs/critias/) slid it forward an inch with two fingers.

"Saturday evening. The Auditorium. Verdi. I have two seats I will not be using. You may use them yourself or take a companion. Your discretion."

[Darius](/darius-cole/) did not open the envelope. He took it. The cardboard was warm from being held. "I'm grateful, sir."

"You should be curious." [Critias](/npcs/critias/) picked up his glass and turned it in the light without drinking. The wine he held was a prop the way [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/)'s decanter had been a prop, the way the closed book of Greek was a prop. Everything in the room was a prop. Only the man holding it was not. "Curiosity is the discipline that has kept me upright through eras that broke better men."

"I'm curious why you would put me in a particular room on a particular night."

"Then you are paying attention." [Critias](/npcs/critias/) set the glass down without drinking. "[Dimitri](/npcs/dimitri/) is no longer my concern. Nine days clear of him. I have shifted from a defensive posture to an offensive one. I have stopped declining seats at certain tables. The next several months will reshape who counts in this city and who does not." He paused, exactly long enough to be deliberate. "I have decided you may count."

"That's a generous decision."

"It is not. It is a deployment."

[Darius](/darius-cole/) held his eye. The Beast in him was quiet here, the way it was quiet around old things. It recognized weight. It did not waste itself.

"What do you want, sir."

"For now? Your presence Saturday. Your attention. And the report you will make to me afterward, whether or not I ask for it."

"Done."

"Mr. Cole." [Critias](/npcs/critias/)'s eyes went a fraction warmer, which on his face was a great deal. "You will be careful with the woman next to you in row eight. She is not what she appears to be."

"They never are."

"Some are less so than others."

He did not elaborate. He did not need to. The envelope was a deployment. The conversation was a deployment. [Darius](/darius-cole/) put the envelope in his inside coat pocket and felt it sit there against his chest like a second heart, the kind that did not beat.

---

*[Gengis's Brewery](/locations/the-brewery/), the West Side. Ten thirty.*

[Sable](/sable-price/) went alone. The brewery smelled of old hops and rust and a kerosene heater on its third refill. [Gengis](/npcs/gengis/) was at the long table with a beer he was not drinking. Two of his nihilists worked the far wall, pretending not to listen.

"Sabbat in [Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/)," he said when she sat down. "That's what they're saying."

"They are."

He turned the bottle on the table. "The Sabbat tell themselves the Beast is freedom. They go in for that little speech, the one about the chains and the cup and the lie of Humanity." His mouth pulled. "You strip Humanity off a man, you don't get a free man. You get a dog. A dog with a long memory."

[Sable](/sable-price/) watched him. The fluorescent in the ceiling above the loading dock was failing in slow pulses, the kind of light that made everyone in a room look exhausted.

"Will the anarchs stand with the Camarilla against them?"

"For [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/)? No. Not for [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/)." [Gengis](/npcs/gengis/) was not a careful talker. He didn't need to be. The room belonged to him. "For [Pilsen](/locations/pilsen/), for Humboldt, for the West Side blocks where my people sleep — yes. We don't fight your war. We fight on our streets. When they cross those streets, we are in it."

"And if the Camarilla wants more than that."

"Then the Camarilla learns what it learned in '68. We are not a militia. We are neighbors who happen to be dead."

[Sable](/sable-price/) thanked him. He did not say *you're welcome.* He nodded the way men nod when they have agreed to a thing they would have agreed to anyway.

---

*[Astor Street Chantry](/locations/tremere-chantry/). Ten forty.*

[Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) stood in the second-floor study with his hands at his sides. [Nicolai](/npcs/nicolai/) sat behind the desk with the cipher folio open. The room was warm in the way [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) had stopped noticing.

"The Prince deferred the toast," [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) said.

"One month."

"Yes."

[Nicolai](/npcs/nicolai/) wrote something on a sheet of paper. Not a long note. Three words, maybe four. He looked up.

"You will be at the Auditorium on Saturday. Both of you. Your coterie. There is a gathering you should observe. I will arrange the seating."

"[Critias](/npcs/critias/) has already offered [Darius](/darius-cole/) two tickets."

The boy's head tilted half a degree. "Then the matter is convergent. Convergence is not coincidence. Note it."

[Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) noted it. He had been noting it since the elevator at Prudential. The same name on two different invitations. A door with two hands pushing it open from the same side.

"The Pyramid receives everything," [Nicolai](/npcs/nicolai/) said. "Especially what does not yet look like anything."

"Understood."

The ward released behind [Tomas](/tomas-navarro/) with a small dry sound, like a page turning. He took the stairs down.

---

[Darius](/darius-cole/) walked north on State at eleven thirty with the Auditorium tickets in his coat. Hessler's face came up in the back of his attention the way a bruise does — not in the first hour, but later, when the body counts the cost. He did not know if Hessler had found his man. He knew he had bought a month, and a month was a long time to keep three Kindred upright in a city that wanted them on their knees.

The decanter on [Lodin](/npcs/lodin/)'s side table had not been opened. The cup had not been poured. The cup, like the wine in [Critias](/npcs/critias/)'s glass and the beer in [Gengis](/npcs/gengis/)'s bottle, was a prop. Only the men holding it were not.

He counted blood as he walked. Nine.
